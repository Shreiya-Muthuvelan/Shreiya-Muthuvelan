### 🔬 Research

**The Aging Challenge: Face Recognition Under Cross-Age Variation**
`Published · IEEE Xplore · Best Paper Award, IEEE ESCI 2026`

Benchmarked four face recognition models — ArcFace, FaceNet, VGGFace, OpenFace — on cross-age datasets (FG-NET, CACD), designing closed-set identification and subject-disjoint verification protocols from scratch, evaluated via AUC and EER across age-bin pairings.

> **Key finding:** VGGFace showed the strongest cross-age robustness; all models degraded sharply across childhood-to-adulthood transitions.

[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/View_Code-181717?style=flat&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/Shreiya-Muthuvelan/cross-age-face-recognition-benchmark)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/IEEE_Xplore-00629B?style=flat&logo=ieee&logoColor=white)](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11493262)

**Current research interest:** continuous fairness monitoring and drift detection in ML-based decision systems (see FairFlow below) — currently being scoped toward a conference submission.
